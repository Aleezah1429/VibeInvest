# Mission

## Why VibeInvest exists

Roughly 70% of Pakistani startups never make it past the idea stage. Founders pour months into pitches that a competent panel would have shredded in 10 minutes — but most founders never get that panel. They get polite no's from investors who don't explain why, or unconditional "this is amazing!" from family.

VibeInvest is the panel. A four-agent AI boardroom that roasts a startup idea the way a real seed-stage committee would — market, money, brand, strategy — and ends with a single number out of 1000 and one of four verdicts: **Invest / Iterate / Pivot / Pass**.

> **Get roasted before you get rejected.**

## Who it's for

- **Pakistani founders** validating ideas before they pitch to angels, accelerators, or family money.
- **University entrepreneurship programs** (LUMS, NUST, IBA, FAST) that need a screening tool.
- **Angels** doing first-pass triage on inbound decks (Phase 3+).

The audience is culturally specific — Urdu and Roman Urdu speakers, PKR economics, Pakistan-grounded competitor data, MoITT alignment. That specificity is the moat. Generic global startup tools cannot replicate it.

## North star

**Brutal honesty + cultural fit, in under 60 seconds, free for founders.**

Every product decision is evaluated against:
1. Does this make the roast more honest, or more polite? (Honest wins.)
2. Does this feel built for Pakistan, or built for San Francisco? (Pakistan wins.)
3. Does this fit in a 3-minute demo? (For Phase 0 only — relaxes after submission.)

## What we are not

- **Not a chatbot.** Agents are sequential and structured, not conversational.
- **Not a generic startup tool.** Cultural specificity is the differentiator — we do not translate the experience to other markets in Phase 0–3.
- **Not nice.** The Skeptic does not sugarcoat. The Munshi does not round numbers up. Polite tools already exist; founders ignore them.
- **Not a SaaS.** Free for founders forever (Aura Card + top 3 fixes). Revenue comes from B2B (investors, incubators), not from squeezing the founder funnel.

## Success looks like

| Horizon | What "won" means |
| --- | --- |
| Phase 0 (hackathon) | Working live demo, judges remember the brand, top-3 finish |
| Phase 1 (Week 2–6) | 100 ideas analyzed by real founders, ≥4/5 session rating |
| Phase 2 (Month 2–3) | 1,000 ideas, 25% return rate, 1 university partnership signed |
| Phase 3 (Month 4–6) | First 50 paying customers across B2B and B2C |
| Phase 4 (Month 7–12) | 10,000 ideas, 3 incubator partnerships, MRR above PKR 2M |
